# Design

Fourteen documents. **Start with [sentinel-overview.md](sentinel-overview.md)**, which is the short form written to solicit feedback on the concepts and Python API. [sentinel.md](sentinel.md) is the full design and the place arguments are made; [sentinel-reference.md](sentinel-reference.md) restates its conclusions as a reference, without the reasoning. Of the remaining eleven, five each take one concern the core design depends on (deployment, the proxy host, development, inspect_core and the generate stages), [sidecar.md](sidecar.md) designs the sidecar that runs a sentinel beside a model proxy and [sidecar-plan.md](sidecar-plan.md) plans its implementation, [pr-series.md](pr-series.md) and [workstreams.md](workstreams.md) track implementation and who works on what, [requirements-aisi-sync-monitor.md](requirements-aisi-sync-monitor.md) maps an external team's requirements onto the plan, and [sub-agents.md](sub-agents.md) covers agents that start other agents.
